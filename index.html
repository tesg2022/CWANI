Who we are

Intelligence that connects climate, water, food and nature.

CWANI is an environmental intelligence initiative focused on translating complex geospatial and environmental data into useful knowledge for research, planning and decision-making.

Our mission

To empower communities, researchers, public institutions and innovators with accessible, science-based intelligence that supports climate adaptation, sustainable resource management and inclusive development.

⌁

Science-led

Grounded in environmental data, modelling and transparent methods.

◎

Geospatial by design

Place-based insights from satellites, maps and spatial analytics.

✳

AI-enabled

Predictive tools that help interpret patterns and assess future scenarios.

↗

Impact-focused

Designed to inform practical choices and measurable outcomes.

Our approach

Built on data. Guided by science. Focused on impact.

CWANI combines complementary methods to turn environmental complexity into insights that people can use.

STEP 01

Observe

Bring together satellite imagery, climate datasets, field observations and relevant spatial information.

STEP 02

Understand

Use GIS, remote sensing and environmental models to identify patterns, risks and relationships.

STEP 03

Predict

Apply appropriate AI and statistical methods to explore trends, forecasts and future scenarios.

STEP 04

Act

Communicate results through maps, dashboards, decision-support tools and practical recommendations.
